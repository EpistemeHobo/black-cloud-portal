# Premises: T2Gen Internal Developer Portal

*Last updated: 2026-10-29*

The beliefs the portal is built on. Every feature should serve at least one of them, and none should work against them.

## 1. Anyone could take over any system from its page alone

Each system's page is a handover guide. The next owner, often a trainee or junior, can learn what it is, where everything is, how to run and change it safely, what will bite them, who to ask, and what's still in progress, without a meeting.

If a new owner can understand a system from its page, so can a reviewer. Leaders and peers still review projects there in their own time, and review outcomes are recorded on the page. Review is one use of the page, not its purpose.

## 2. Developers write only what helps the next owner

Developers are asked to answer the [seven handover questions](handover-guide.md) and nothing else. Catalog details for audit (cost, contracts, data classification and the like) live in a separate form that audit or IT staff fill in.

Writing and reading must both be easy: short questions with examples, edits in place, and a page that answers the basics on its first screen.
